Liquid Mutual Fund Graded Exit Load Schedule

exp_fee_liquid_01

Category: Mutual Funds · SEBI Regulations

active reuse count: 342 last checked: 2024-10-18
fact_check Canonical Facts (Grounding Rules)
  1. 1 Graded exit load is mandatory across all Indian mutual fund AMCs per SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/DF2/CIR/P/2019/101.
  2. 2 Exit load starts at 0.0070% on Day 1 and gradually decreases each 24-hour cycle until Day 7.
  3. 3
    Standard 7-Day Graded Tier Scale:
    Day 1: 0.0070% Day 2: 0.0065% Day 3: 0.0060% Day 4: 0.0055% Day 5: 0.0050% Day 6: 0.0045%
  4. 4 Redemptions executed on or after the 8th calendar day from unit allotment carry 0.00% (nil) exit load.
  5. 5 The exit load amount is credited back into the scheme's assets (AUM) for the benefit of remaining unitholders, not retained by Groww.

Zero Brokerage and Depository Participant (DP) Charges for Mutual Funds

exp_fee_brokerage_mf_02

Category: Direct Plans · Pricing

active reuse count: 819 last checked: 2024-10-15
fact_check Canonical Facts (Grounding Rules)
  1. 1 Groww charges ₹0 (zero) brokerage and ₹0 commission on all Direct Mutual Fund investments and redemptions.
  2. 2 There are no annual account maintenance charges (AMC) levied on direct mutual fund holdings in Groww.
  3. 3 Standard Depository Participant (DP) charges do not apply to mutual fund redemptions processed via AMC registrar (CAMS/KFintech).
  4. 4 Stamp duty of 0.005% is deducted at purchase in accordance with the Indian Stamp Act amendments.
