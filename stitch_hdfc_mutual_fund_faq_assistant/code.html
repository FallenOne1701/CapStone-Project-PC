What is the exit load for HDFC Mid Cap Fund?
HF
Factual HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund · Direct Plan
Section 1.3 SID

For units redeemed or switched out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment, the exit load is 1.00%.

No exit load is payable if units are redeemed or switched out after completion of 1 year from allotment date.

View source: HDFC Scheme Information Document (SID, Page 42) Last updated from sources: 17 Nov 2026
